Help and support

Is talking to an expert really free?

Yes. Expert calls are free on every plan. Reviews and direct-plan advice are part of Track B.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Advisory reviews pause after the due date. The app, portfolio view and expert calls stay on. Pay any time to resume.

How do I change my plan?

Open Profile, then App fee and plans. Changes apply at the end of your billing cycle.
